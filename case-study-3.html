Brand & Web

Field Notes Studio

Identity and a marketing site for a small creative studio that wanted to feel handmade — without becoming a maintenance headache.

Role
Designer & Front-end
Timeline
6 weeks · 2023
Deliverables
Brand, website, guidelines

01 — Overview

A studio that made warm things, with a cold website.

Field Notes is a three-person studio doing beautiful, tactile work — risograph prints, hand-bound books, slow branding projects. Their old site was a stiff template that made all of it look generic. New clients couldn’t tell what made them different, because nothing on the page did.

02 — Problem

Handmade warmth that a three-person team could keep up.

The brief had a built-in tension. The studio wanted the site to feel personal and a little imperfect — but they had no one to babysit a fragile, hand-tuned page. Whatever I made had to survive being updated by a busy founder on a Sunday night.

Handmade doesn’t have to mean inconsistent.

The answer was to put the personality into a small, reusable system: a warm palette, an expressive display face, and a set of layout patterns with deliberate asymmetry baked in. Character by design, not by hand.

03 — Process

A kit of parts with built-in personality.

I built the identity around a cream-and-terracotta palette and a pairing of a characterful serif with a calm humanist sans. Then I translated it into a handful of flexible page blocks — a hero, a project strip, a wide image, a quote — that the team could recombine without ever landing on something off-brand.

I hand-coded the site as flat HTML and CSS so there was no build step and nothing to break — every editable bit lives in plain markup, with a short guide written in the studio’s own voice.

04 — Outcome

A site that finally looks like the work.

The new identity launched alongside a site the founders can update themselves. In the first three months, enquiries through the contact page roughly doubled, and — their favorite metric — two new clients said they got in touch because the site “felt like a real person made it.”

Brand system and marketing site — built to be recombined, not redrawn.
