About

Karina Vega

UI/UX designer focused on clarity, craft, and the small decisions that make a product feel trustworthy.

I’m a product designer with six years of experience spanning fintech, developer tools, and small creative studios. I work end to end — research, interaction design, prototyping, and the hands-on detail of shipping with engineers — but I’m happiest in the messy middle, turning a vague brief into something concrete enough to argue about.

My approach is systems first, pixels second. Before I push a single rectangle around, I want to understand the shape of the problem: who’s using this, what they’re afraid of, and where the current experience quietly lets them down. Good interfaces are mostly good decisions made early and held onto.

I came to design sideways — a degree in linguistics, a few years writing documentation, and a stubborn habit of redrawing every form I had to fill out. That background still shows up in how I work: I treat words as part of the interface, and I sweat the empty states as much as the happy path.
